The daily brief for
early‑stage founders.

It reads what your company actually did yesterday and tells you the three things that matter today. One page. One email. Thirty seconds.

Micah Okoko · micahokoko@gmail.com

The problem

Founders open six tools every morning and still cannot say what changed.

GitHub knows what shipped. Stripe knows what was paid. The database knows who signed up. Analytics knows who visited. Each one answers its own question. None of them answer the founder’s: what happened, and what do I do about it.

So the check gets skipped. A regression sits for eleven days. A channel that was working stops working and nobody notices until the month closes.

The solution

A wire dispatch about your own company, every morning.

Signups +18% 47
Pull requests merged — 6
Activation −7% 29%

Activation fell the day the onboarding form shipped. Roll it back, or split the step.

No dashboard. No tabs. Nothing to monitor. Something you finish reading.

How it works

Built. Running nightly.

collectGitHub — merged PRs, commits, deploys, open PRs
collectSupabase — signups, from a table you map
collectPlausible — visitors, referrers
collectStripe — revenue, new customers
diffdeterministic engine — deltas, streaks, gaps
polishmodel rewrites the prose — prose only
validateevery figure must exist in the facts
deliverone page, one email

Setup is fifteen minutes. The founder’s database is queried with counts only — row contents are never read and never leave their infrastructure.

The insight

The model never originates a number.

Every figure is computed by a deterministic engine, then validated against the source facts before it ships. The language model is allowed to write sentences and nothing else. When a cause is not in the data, the brief says cause unknown from connected data.

An AI analyst that is wrong once is deleted. Trust is not a feature of this product. It is the product.

Why we win the entry point

Every founder tool assumes revenue already exists. Ours doesn’t.

Mixpanel, Amplitude and Databox need event volume to be useful. A pre‑product‑market‑fit founder has commits, deploys, a handful of signups and a landing page — which is exactly the data Founder Brief reads.

We are useful on day one of a company, arrive before the analytics decision is made, and are already installed when it is. The incumbents cannot follow down‑market without becoming a worse version of themselves.

Those tools show data. This shows decisions. Different category, different buyer, same founder.

Why it compounds

A daily habit that accumulates a record no competitor can reconstruct.

  1. 01Every brief is an event and an outcome, dated. Over time that is a labelled history of what changes moved which metric.
  2. 02Across companies it becomes a benchmark: your activation is 32%, comparable startups run 41%. No single founder can build that.
  3. 03The history, the metric mapping and the connected integrations do not export. Leaving costs the founder their record.

The first version is easy to copy. The third year is not. The asset is the outcome data, not the daily email.
